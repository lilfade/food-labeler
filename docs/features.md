# Everything Food Labeler does

[Back to the start](../README.md)

A plain list of what's in the app. Free, no account, works without internet.

## Making labels
- **Kinds of label:** simple food label, cottage food label, package label,
  date sticker, free text, and a picture label (logo sticker or round seal).
- **Label sizes:** small 32 × 25 mm, 3 × 2 in, 4 × 2 in, 2 in round and
  4 × 6 in. Every size except round can be laid out **wide or tall**.
- **What you see is what prints:** the preview is the exact picture sent to
  the printer.
- **Dates made easy:** *Made on* is today; *Use by* with quick buttons
  (+3 days, +1 week, +1 month…) or a calendar.
- **Allergens:** tap the major allergens (milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, tree
  nuts, peanuts, wheat, soy, sesame) and the *Contains:* line fills in.
- **Too much writing?** It shrinks the text to fit and tells you plainly when
  something won't fit, instead of cutting it off.
- **How many labels:** big − and + buttons, then one green **PRINT** button.

## For home bakers (cottage food)
- **Your state's wording** for all 50 states and Washington, D.C., each with
  its official source and the date it was checked; states we couldn't confirm
  say so.
- **Label check:** a green line when everything your state asks for is on the
  label, or a list of what's missing. Required wording is never shrunk below
  your state's minimum size.
- **My bakery:** your business name, address, phone, email and permit number,
  typed once. Picking your state fills in the State part of the address.
- **Your wording is kept:** change any statement; app updates never overwrite
  your changes, and you can reset to the state's suggestion.
- **My ingredients:** ingredients with what's in them ("Butter (cream, salt)")
  and their allergens, put in order heaviest first.
- **My products:** save a product once and it's one click next time, right at
  the top of the window. Search, favourites (★) and recently used first.

## Make it yours
- **Lettering:** 8 built-in fonts plus any Google Font by name; fancy
  lettering for the food's name, while the wording the law asks for always
  stays easy to read.
- **Pictures and logos:** PNG, JPG, GIF, BMP, WebP, TIFF; turned into black
  and white for the printer (solid for logos, tiny dots for photos), with a
  warning if it would print mostly black. Above, below, left, right or in any
  corner. **My pictures** keeps your logo one click away.
- **QR codes:** your website, phone number, email or a note (like how to
  reheat it), with words under it. Sizes are limited to ones phones can scan.
- **Save as picture** to share a label, and **Undo** for the last change.

## Printers
- **Tested on paper:** LP610BT-type label printers (sold as Labelnize,
  Flexyva, Polono and others) over USB and over the network, and the Zebra
  ZP450 over USB.
- **Alpha (should work, not tested yet):** MUNBYN, Rollo, iDPRT/HPRT, Polono
  PL60 and PL70e, Phomemo, Jadens/Beeprt, Xprinter, Arkscan and other Zebras.
  See [Printers we know about](printer-list.md).
- **Connections:** USB cable, network (print server or bridge), and
  Bluetooth (alpha) on Windows and Linux.
- **Several printers at once:** pick from a list, give them names, and print a
  "Which one is it?" label to tell identical printers apart.
- **New roll? Set it up:** calibrates the printer for the roll and prints a
  line-up test label.
- **Print settings per printer:** darker or lighter, slower or faster, nudge
  the print, upside down, labels with gaps / black marks / continuous roll,
  tear-off, a darkness test, feed one blank label.
- **Normal (203 dpi) or sharp (300 dpi)** printers.
- **Printer not listed?** A window shows what's plugged in, and help is one
  click away (the printer report is copied for you, with no label contents).

## Everyday comfort
- **Text size:** Small, Normal, Large or Extra large.
- **Your work is kept safe** automatically and opens where you left off.
- **Open ▾** lists your recent labels; label files are yours, in a folder on
  your computer.
- **Updates you can trust:** printer and state-rule update files are checked
  before they're used, and your own settings always survive an update.
- **Private:** no account, no tracking; the only optional internet use is
  fetching a Google Font when you ask for one.

# Why we made this

[← Back to the start](../README.md)

We keep seeing the same thing happen. Someone starts a small business —
cookies, jam, candles, soap — and buys a cheap label printer to make it look
neat and do things properly. Then the printer turns into a project of its
own: drivers, apps, settings, label sizes that don't line up. Evenings go
into learning tech skills nobody asked for, just to make a little money from
something they love. By the end it can feel like a waste.

We're tech people, and we had already solved these problems for ourselves.
Something like this shouldn't cost money, so we see no reason to charge for
it. Food Labeler is free, and it stays free.

## Your printer, your labels

Nobody starting out knows exactly what they will need. That shouldn't lock
you into one product, or into one company's app, for as long as you run your
business.

- **Many printers.** Food Labeler works with more than one kind of label
  printer, and we add more as people tell us about theirs.
- **Your own computer.** It prints from your Windows or Linux computer, and it
  works without the internet.
- **No account, no subscription.** There is nothing to sign up for and
  nothing to pay, now or later.
- **Your files are yours.** Your labels are ordinary files in a folder on your
  computer (*Documents → My Food Labels*). Copy them, back them up or take
  them with you.

## Same printer, different names

Many cheap label printers are really the same printer, sold under different
names. Often the only way to use one is the seller's phone app. Food Labeler
talks to the printer directly instead, so it doesn't matter whose name is on
the box. See [Printers we know about](printer-list.md) for the ones we know
so far, and which we have tested on paper.

## Tell us how it goes

If your printer isn't working, or Food Labeler is confusing, please
[open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/lilfade/food-labeler/issues/new/choose)
and we'll try to help. It's free to make a GitHub account; or, if you're
stuck, email hello@highthymekitchen.com.

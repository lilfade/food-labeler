How it works

  1. Plug in your printer

    Connect a small sticker printer to your computer with its USB cable and switch it on.

  2. Type your food

    Open Food Labeler and type the name of your food. Today's date is already filled in.

  3. Press PRINT

    Press the big green PRINT button. Out comes your sticker. What you see on the screen is exactly what prints.

Made for home bakers

If you sell baked goods from home under your state's cottage food law, choose Cottage food label. Food Labeler helps you get the label right.

  • Your state's wording. All 50 states and Washington, D.C. Each one shows the official source it comes from and the date it was checked. If a state could not be confirmed, it says so.
  • Ingredients and allergens. Pick from My ingredients, heaviest first. The allergens fill themselves in.
  • Label check. A green line says when everything your state asks for is on the label. If something is missing, it tells you what.
  • My products. Save a product once. Next time, one click fills in the whole label.
  • Your own wording is kept. You can change the wording. New versions of Food Labeler never overwrite your changes.

Please note: the wording comes from public state guidance and is a suggestion. You are responsible for following your state's rules.

Make it yours

  • Lettering. Pick fancy letters for the name of your food, like Pacifico or Caveat. The words the law asks for always stay clear and easy to read.
  • Pictures and logos. Add your logo or any picture. Food Labeler turns colour pictures into black and white, because label printers only print black.
  • QR codes. Add a code people scan with their phone: your website, phone number, email, or a note like how to reheat it.

Works with your printer

  • LP610BT-type label printers, sold as Labelnize, Flexyva, Polono and others.
  • Zebra label printers, such as the ZP450.
  • Plugged in with a USB cable, on Windows or Linux.
  • Normal (203 dpi) or sharp (300 dpi) printers.

Label sizes

New roll? Set it up

When you put in a new roll, Food Labeler walks you through setting it up in three plain steps, so the printing lands on the labels and not across the gaps.

Printer not in the list?

Press My printer isn't in the list. Food Labeler can make a short report about your computer and printer (never what is on your labels) for you to send to us, and we will help.

Why not just use a website?

Label websites

Many know the wording a food label needs. But they print through your web browser, and small sticker printers often come out the wrong size, with odd margins or cut off.

General label programs

They talk to your printer well. But they don't know food labels or your state's rules, so you have to work out the wording yourself.

Food Labeler

Does both. It knows the wording and prints straight to your label printer at the right size. It all works on your own computer, without internet.

First time on Windows

The first time you open Food Labeler, Windows may show a blue box that says “Windows protected your PC”. That is normal for a small program that isn't sold in a shop. It does not mean anything is wrong.

  1. Click the words More info.

    Windows protected your PC

    Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognized app from starting…

    More info

    Don't run

  2. Then click the Run anyway button that appears.

    Windows protected your PC

    App: FoodLabeler.exe

    Publisher: Unknown publisher

    Run anyway Don't run

Windows usually asks this only the first time.

Stay safe: only download Food Labeler from github.com/lilfade/food-labeler/releases, never from a link someone sends you.
